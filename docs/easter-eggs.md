# Easter eggs on aaryush.com

These are hidden details for curious visitors. The rule is that they're **shown, never explained**. Nothing on the site points at them or describes them.

**Status:** ✅ built · 🔨 queued

| Easter egg | Where | How you find it | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The logo draws itself** | Header, every page | The "ag." writes on once per visit, and hovering replays it. | ✅ |
| **The drawing title block** | Home hero, desktop | Dwg. no. AG-001 · Sheet 01 / 05 · Region us-central · Last deploy (stamped at build) · Scale NTS. These are drafting jokes for anyone who's read drawings. | ✅ |
| **The hero keeps your clock** | Home hero | The topography changes with your local time: cool in the morning, warm in the evening, sparse and slow at night. | ✅ |
| **The writing room keeps your clock** | /writing | Morning window light, a softer afternoon, warm evening light, and only the lamp-light pool at night. | ✅ |
| **Time preview** | Home and /writing | Press `t` to step through morning, afternoon, evening, night and back to auto. The override carries across pages for the session. | ✅ |
| **Keyboard map** | Every page | Press `?`. | ✅ |
| **Section shortcuts** | Every page | `g h` hero · `g l` How AI fits · `g w` work · `g r` writing · `g c` contact. | ✅ |
| **Console note** | DevTools, every page | How this site was built, as a conversation. A note signed "— ag.", with the loop drawn in ASCII. | ✅ |
| **404 as a missing sheet** | Any bad URL | "Sheet not in set." The requested path is struck through in the drawing register as "Not issued". | ✅ |
| **Print as a drawing sheet** | Home, Ctrl+P | Prints a black-on-white drawing sheet with a title block. Contact details are assembled only while printing. | ✅ |
| **A contact card that remembers where you met** | /qr, then Save contact | You type where you met and tap what you talked about. The QR redraws live, and the scanner's saved contact gets a note: "Met at … · date · Talked about …". | ✅ |
| **Shared-letter morph glow** | Home hero | Letters shared between lines glide, and new ones arrive amber. Small changes glow faintly, and the main word glows fully. | ✅ |
| **Dust in the light** | /writing | Slow motes drift, but only inside the pool of light. | ✅ |
| **"you're up late too."** | /writing, 9pm–7am | A handwritten note on the desk that catches the light. It's absent in the daytime. | ✅ |
| **The drawer** | /writing | A small, quiet drawer that slides open to a contextOS folder. | ✅ |
| **Every piece signs itself** | Essay pages (letters, papers) | At the end of every piece, the "ag." signature draws itself. | ✅ |
| **The room remembers you** | /writing and the home teaser | Pieces you've opened sit slightly pulled out, with a mark at the part you reached: an amber sticky flag on a paper, a faint amber thumbprint on a letter's fold, and the card you reached pulled out of a note's stack. "Pick up where you left off" on the page, and on the home teaser a "Keep reading · section III" on each started piece (plus a named "Pick up *Title*" for one the teaser doesn't list). It's stored only in your own browser. | ✅ |
| **General notes** | Home hero, 9pm–7am (under the contact buttons on phones) | Pencilled "GENERAL NOTES 1. It's past nine. 2. You're still here. 3. Good." | ✅ |
| **contextOS caret** | Home timeline, "Next" line | An amber caret blinks where the period was, as if the name is still being typed. | ✅ |
| **Type `contextos`** | Home, anywhere outside text fields | The contextOS word re-types itself once with the hero's morph. | ✅ |
| **"Save contact" becomes "Saved."** | Home hero and footer | After the vCard downloads, the label morphs to "Saved." with an amber hairline and a check. Back to normal on the next page load. | ✅ |
| **The deploy hash** | Home hero title block, desktop | Hover or focus "Last deploy" to see the commit it was built from. | ✅ |
| **Type `ag.`** | Every page, outside text fields | The header logo draws itself on again. | ✅ |
| **The lamp warms while you read** | Essay pages | The warm light behind the page grows, very slowly, the longer you read. It never touches the text. | ✅ |
| **Signed prints** | Home, Ctrl+P | The printed drawing sheet carries a small pencil "ag." in its title block. | ✅ |
| **The revision block** | Home hero, desktop | Click (or Tab to and press Enter on) the "Last deploy" cell of the title block. A drawing-style revision block opens with Rev · Date · Description, newest first. Esc, an outside click, or a second click closes it. | ✅ |
| **Arrow keys walk the desk** | /writing | With no text field focused, ← and → move the light and the keyboard focus from piece to piece, Home and End jump to the ends, and Enter opens the lit piece. Under reduced motion the light jumps. | ✅ |
| **The writing room's own 404** | /writing/anything-unknown | The dim desk in the current daypart's light, one crumpled sheet in the pool of light, and a quiet "Back to the desk". The status is a real 404. | ✅ |
