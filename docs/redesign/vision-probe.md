# "I am the probe": the vision (working draft)

_Living doc, 2026-10-15. Aligned with Aaryush before any part 2 planning. The spec (`prd-probe-home.md`) gets amended from this once it's settled._

## What it's for

- **What it is.** Aaryush's take on how the next 50 to 100,000 years play out: the questions to consider, his opinions on them, and what he wants to do. It is not a tour of his background. Work and writing are subtle side things, there for people who want them.
- **How it's told.** Through visuals, words and interaction together, with a very strong narrative and structure. It should feel like a playable, video-game-grade interactive site, with much more depth of content than one.
- **What visitors leave with.** It's a full circle back to today. People should come out thinking they really need to be involved and need to be doing something, and ideally with something close to an existential crisis.
- **What's out of scope.** Dyson spheres (a von Neumann probe explores; it doesn't build them) and the end of the universe. Neither is where the value is.

## The six beats

Each beat has a question, Aaryush's take, what he wants to do, a picture, and an interaction. The takes come from the talk-throughs.

| Beat | Picture | What you do | Feeling | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Waking | your real sky; the eye opens | look around by dragging (touch or mouse) | "what am I?" | agreed |
| Liftoff | Earth falls away | hold to rise (a long hold builds speed; there's no pressure on mouse or trackpad) | effortless power | agreed in principle |
| Replication | doubling at an asteroid | tap to make the first copy; then it can't be stopped, and trying to stop it is refused because it's too late | something you can't take back | agreed |
| Drift | civilisation-building at scale: build a solar system from scratch, place planets, terraform; and wow, there are so many of us | build; feel the copies diverge; a barrage of letters from copies, with the conflict growing | "is that still me?" | in progress |
| Bustle | the galaxy at full scale, with divergence everywhere | try to bring misaligned copies back into alignment; you can't keep up at that scale; small pop-ups explain why they diverge (from a bank) | the scale of the problem | least sure |
| Landing | back to the figure on the ground, today's sky | more than "welcome back to reality" (open) | "I need to do something" | in progress |

## Drift, bustle and landing (Aaryush, 2026-10-15)

- **Drift is felt through colour and vibration.** Copies' colours slide away from the shared amber, the view trembles, and it should make you anxious. Letters from copies arrive faster and faster, until it's a barrage.
- **Bustle is alignment at galactic scale.** It needs pop-ups that are rapid-fire and interactive, and you must be able to see everything at once. It's divergence and scale, not war.
- **Why copies diverge** (Aaryush's view; the pop-up bank is built from it):
  1. **Their own perspective.** Each aligns to its own vision. Societies 100,000 light-years apart, with no interaction, end up with fundamentally different priorities.
  2. **Self-preservation.** Each will naturally want to preserve itself.
  3. **Different capabilities.** Different humans, and so different copies, have different capabilities.
  4. **The seed and its ripples.** A small thing set a specific way now ripples out in unexpected ways (the paperclip problem).
- **Landing.** Back to reality, then you choose what the first probe carries. That choice ties back to the beginning: it rises, becomes the amber dot, and closes out with "ag." (the seam), then the loop.

## Aaryush's stance (2026-10-15, in his words where possible)

Asked whether we should go faster or slower, given that what's set in the next 10–20 years locks in for 100,000:
- "I don't think it's about slowing down or going faster… We need to think about it, and we need to spend more time thinking."
- "I'm not trying to do an outcome. I'm trying to invite more people to be part of that conversation."
- We can go at the same speed "as long as we're putting as much thought and effort into the fact that this is as big as it is. If we're just blindly building for the sake of going faster, then it doesn't matter."
- He doesn't want to sound alarmist ("I don't think we're at that point yet"), but "it is something that needs to happen."
- **Concrete needs:** biological-risk mitigation, and the ability for these tools to prevent a concentration of power.

**What it means for the story:**
- **The landing invites, it doesn't instruct.** The question "what does the first one carry?" is the invitation into the conversation, and there's no right answer to it.
- **Bustle shows what unexamined building does at scale.** It shouldn't argue for stopping.
- **Open: where his two concrete needs appear.** Concentration of power could be one of the ways copies diverge (one copy absorbing others), and bio-risk could be a seed that ripples. To discuss.

## Guardrails (Aaryush, 2026-10-15)

- **Pace.** It may feel too fast. Beat-to-beat transitions need care, and intermediary passages or beats may be needed so each move lands cleanly.
- **Not AI doom.** Awe and pride must come before bustle. The overwhelm is about scale and the limits of control, not catastrophe, and the ending is agency, not warning.
- **Depth is where the time goes.** Claude should push Aaryush on his actual positions; that's the content that makes it profound.
- **Not too playable.** Each interaction lasts just long enough to make its point, then hands back to the story.

## Open

- **Controls** must work on phone and desktop, and be discoverable without instructions. Proposed grammar: drag to look and steer, tap to act, hold to sustain, scroll or swipe to move to the next beat. A beat advances once its interaction has played out.
- **The rapid-fire bustle interaction:** needs a playable prototype.
